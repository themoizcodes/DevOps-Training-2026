Ab mein yeh chah raha hn kay permissions ka access dena hai then command huti hai 

chmod

chmod 744

chmod command accepts krta hai numerical values na ky rwx 


read ki numerical value 4 
write  ki numerical value 2
execute  ki  numerical value 1

Total 7 

rwx = 7
rw- = 6
r-x = 5
r-- = 4

Ab chmod 744 means 
Owner uSer ko rwx read,write,execute krskta 
Owner Group ko r-- read krskta hai 
Others ko r-- read krskta hai 

Puri command:
chmod 744 newfile.txt
Then mein check krun permission ls -l krun then mein ne jo permissions rkhi thi woh update hujayein gi
